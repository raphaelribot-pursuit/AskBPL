F. Library Resources. Covered Persons should not use their position to obtain
unreasonable or excessive services or expertise from Library staff. Covered
Persons shall use Library resources for Library business only and not for
personal gain or at the expense of patrons unless disclosed to and cleared by
the Audit Committee or by Independent Trustees on the Board of Trustees,
provided, however, that they may use Library resources in the same manner
as any member of the general public. Library resources include, but are not
limited to, the Library’s collections, supplies, equipment, staff and premises.
In addition, the name, reputation and goodwill of the Library are valuable
assets and should not be used by Trustees and BPL employees for personal
advantage or for the advantage of any other person or entity.
