# Gift Acceptance Policy

## Purpose

Brooklyn Public Library accepts philanthropic gifts that advance its mission while protecting donor intent and institutional integrity.

## Approval Thresholds

Gifts valued at five thousand dollars ($5,000) or less may be accepted by branch managers with a completed intake form. Gifts above five thousand dollars require Director-level approval and a written summary placed on the donor record before public acknowledgment.

## Prohibited Gifts

Cash gifts without receipt documentation, gifts that create ongoing financial obligations, and gifts that appear to influence collection decisions are prohibited.

## Disclosure Requirements

Staff must disclose any personal relationship with a prospective donor before accepting a gift on behalf of the Library.
