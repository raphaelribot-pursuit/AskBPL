# Employee Conduct Guidelines

## Professional Standards

All staff represent Brooklyn Public Library in public and digital spaces. Courtesy, equity, and confidentiality are baseline expectations.

## Conflicts of Interest

Staff must recuse themselves from decisions where personal financial interest could influence outcomes. Report potential conflicts to a supervisor before proceeding.

## Use of Library Resources

Library equipment and accounts are for mission-related work. Incidental personal use must not interfere with service delivery or violate policy.

## Reporting Concerns

Ethics questions without a clear policy answer should be escalated to a supervisor or Human Resources rather than resolved informally.
