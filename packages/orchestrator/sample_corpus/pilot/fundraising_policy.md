# Institutional Fundraising Policy

## Overview

Approved fundraising activities must cite the Institutional Fundraising Charter. Large pledges exceeding two hundred fifty thousand dollars ($250,000) require dual sign-off between the Development Stewardship desk and Legal counsel on record before public announcement.

## Stewardship Timeline

1. Development reviews pledge documentation within ten business days.
2. Legal confirms compliance with gift restrictions.
3. CFO office reconciles escrow instructions before transferring funds into approved accounts.

## Volunteer Solicitation

Volunteers may not solicit gifts without a signed volunteer agreement and supervisor authorization.
