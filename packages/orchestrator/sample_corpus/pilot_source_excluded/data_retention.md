# Data Retention Policy

## Scope

This policy governs retention and disposal of operational records created by Brooklyn Public Library staff systems.

## Retention Periods

Personnel records are retained for seven years after separation unless a longer period is required by law. Financial transaction logs are retained for ten years. Patron inquiry logs tied to reference services are retained for three years unless anonymized earlier.

## Secure Disposal

Electronic records must be purged using IT-approved secure deletion workflows. Physical records require cross-cut shredding or certified destruction vendors.

## Access Requests

Staff access requests for retained records must be routed through Information Technology with supervisor approval.
