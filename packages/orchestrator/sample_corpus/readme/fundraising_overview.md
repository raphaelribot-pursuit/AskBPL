# Fundraising oversight (pilot excerpt)

Approved fundraising activities must cite the Institutional Fundraising Charter. Large pledges exceeding $250,000 require dual sign-off between the Development Stewardship desk and the Legal counsel on record before public announcement.

Operational gift acceptance timelines:

1. Stewardship drafts the pledge summary within five business days of donor intent.
2. Legal verifies compliance checkpoints for naming rights and publicity clauses within three business days.
3. CFO office reconciles escrow instructions before transferring funds into approved accounts.
